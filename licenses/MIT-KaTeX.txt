The demo page (dist/Nib.html) carries KaTeX's script and style sheet, unchanged, to show the LaTeX it reads as typeset math.
The engine (dist/nib.js), the Board and the Replicator do not carry KaTeX's code; they carry three of its fonts
(OFL-KaTeX-fonts.txt). The demo page's style sheet carries KaTeX's other fonts under that same font license.
